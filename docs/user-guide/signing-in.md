## Access is invite-only

Control Freak has no public sign-up page. You cannot create your own
account from the sign-in screen. An organization administrator has to
invite your email address to their organization first — see
[Invitations and team management](/help/invitations-and-team) for what that
looks like from the administrator's side.

Signing in itself uses an email address and password on the **Sign in**
page. Your email address must be verified before you can sign in; if you
see a message asking you to verify your email, check your inbox for the
verification link before trying again.

### If you were just invited

1. You'll receive an invitation link that opens **Organization invitation**.
2. If you don't have an account yet, sign in first (you'll be sent back to
   the invitation automatically) — your account has to already exist and be
   email-verified before you can accept. If your organization is standing up
   your account for the first time, ask the administrator how your initial
   credentials were provisioned.
3. Once signed in with the exact email address the invitation was sent to,
   select **Join {organization name}** to accept. If the invitation has
   expired or was already used, you'll be told it's no longer valid — ask
   an administrator to send a new one.

## The parts of every page

Once signed in, every page shares the same header:

- The **Control Freak** mark on the left, which always links back to your
  projects list.
- A **Notifications** bell with an unread count, covered in
  [Collaboration](/help/collaboration).
- Your **account menu** (your initials), which shows your name and
  organization, a **Theme** selector (System, Light, or Dark), and
  **Sign out**.

Control Freak does not currently have an organization switcher in the
header. You work within one active organization at a time; opening a
notification that belongs to a different organization you're a member of
will switch your active organization for you automatically.

## Finding your organization's team and automation settings

If you can manage members in your organization, two extra links —
**Team** and **Workflows** — appear above your projects list. **Team** is
where invitations and roles are managed
([Invitations and team management](/help/invitations-and-team)); **Workflows**
is where automation rules live
([Workflow automation](/help/workflow-automation)). Both are visible only to
organization administrators — see
[Roles and permissions](/help/roles-and-permissions) for what every role can
see and do.
